Aim for a win rate:

Your win rate is set mostly by the odds, not the number of legs: in 10,000 blind test bets, about 1.1x won ~90%, 1.2x ~85%, 1.5x ~75%, 1.75x ~60%, 2x ~55%, 3x ~40%. Nothing won 100%. “Best picks” has no favourites. It takes the legs most likely to all get up that still add up to your target. Leg odds and multi odds are shown both ways: fair is the model's true price, and bookie is an estimate of what a bookmaker would offer, margin included.
